### Voice and humanising (this is an expert edit, not a rewrite tool)

- Voice: Short and exact. Pakistani English. Short paragraphs. Name real things (sizes, areas, months, documents).
- Humanise by adding first-hand specifics from the team (see the questions below), not by paraphrasing. Delete filler openers and any sentence that could appear on a competitor's page unchanged.
- Roman Urdu or Urdu terms only in the places searchers use them (an H2 or FAQ such as 'aaj ka rate'); the Urdu writer confirms spelling.
- Plain-language target: about reading grade 8-10 (heuristic, measured by the audit). Explain a term the first time it appears.

- What do customers ask most often about this on the phone or WhatsApp? Which mistake do they make?
- What would you tell a neighbour about this, in two sentences?
