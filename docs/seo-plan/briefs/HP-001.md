### Must NOT claim

- 'cheapest', 'best price', 'fastest', '#1', 'lowest price' or any superlative without proof
- a delivery time (30 minutes, 1 hour) unless it was measured in that city in a real test order, with the date
- years in business, customers served, orders delivered, or ratings and reviews, unless documented
- any office, branch or 'local team' outside Islamabad G-12
- partner names, phone numbers or addresses without their written consent
- 'reviewed by' or a review date before Mr Rasheed has signed off that page
- a price that is not in an OGRA notification we checked, or a delivery charge we have not agreed
- 'free', 'guaranteed' or 'no fee' anywhere unless the owner confirms it as a standing policy
- competitor names, SERP notes, tool output or the word 'keyword' on a published page

### Voice and humanising (this is an expert edit, not a rewrite tool)

- Voice: Direct, warm, first person plural; no slogans. Pakistani English. Short paragraphs. Name real things (sizes, areas, months, documents).
- Humanise by adding first-hand specifics from the team (see the questions below), not by paraphrasing. Delete filler openers and any sentence that could appear on a competitor's page unchanged.
- Roman Urdu or Urdu terms only in the places searchers use them (an H2 or FAQ such as 'aaj ka rate'); the Urdu writer confirms spelling.
- Plain-language target: about reading grade 8-10 (heuristic, measured by the audit). Explain a term the first time it appears.

**Questions to ask the team before writing (so the page contains first-hand detail):**

- What exactly is true for: licence scan? Is there a document, photo or number we can show?
- What exactly is true for: latest ogra notification? Is there a document, photo or number we can show?
- What exactly is true for: photos? Is there a document, photo or number we can show?
- What do customers ask most often about this on the phone or WhatsApp? Which mistake do they make?
- What would you tell a neighbour about this, in two sentences?

### CTA and conversion

- Call, WhatsApp and the 4-field form above the fold; sticky bar on mobile.
- Disclosure near any form: who will call, why, and that the order may be fulfilled by a partner (consent text version is stored with the lead).

### Media

- Real office photo
- Cylinder with OGRA marking (close-up)
- Delivery photo with consent
- Filenames: `lpg-<what-it-shows>.webp` (lowercase, hyphens). Alt text pattern: *Lpg: <what the image shows, in one plain sentence>* (describes the image; the keyword only if it fits naturally). No stock photos of people or workers.
- Compress images; set width and height to avoid layout shift. No images of text where HTML text works.

### Length (information, not a target)

Matching competitor pages in the crawl run 868-1735 words (median 1016; 8 pages, JS-rendered pages excluded). Google has no preferred length. The page is finished when every question in the outline is answered with a fact, example or action, and nothing is repeated for padding.
