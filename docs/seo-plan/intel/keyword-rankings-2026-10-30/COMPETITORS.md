## 3. What the winners do that you can copy

1. **Date in the title.** icons.com.pk ("Today (28-10-2026)") and sui-gas.pk keep the date visible. Your titles show the month ("Oct 2026"), which is good. Keep it updated on the 1st.
2. **A long price history.** ceicdata and karandaaz rank only because of their time series. Your history page has 2 months.
3. **Product pages for cylinders.** burhangas (#1 "lpg cylinder") and PARCO rank with a single product page per cylinder. Your `/lpg-cylinders/...` pages are similar; they need photos, price and an "order" button above the fold.
4. **Social posts on the day of the price change.** Facebook and YouTube posts rank in the top 3 within days.
5. **Order forms rank for price searches.** PSO's #1 is an order form. Google treats "lpg price" as "I want to buy", so your price pages should make ordering obvious.
