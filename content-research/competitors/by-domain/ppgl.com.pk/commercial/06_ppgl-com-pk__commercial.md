Safety Guidelines for the HoReCa Segment Storage and Installation Ensure the LPG tanks are installed in well-ventilated, outdoor areas away from direct sunlight, high traffic, or heat sources. Ensure safe distance is maintained from flammable materials, electrical installations, and open flames as per local regulations. Ensure the integrity of the cylinders/tanks installed at site with proper 3rd party certificates. Use soapy water to detect
leaks. Never use a flame. Install gas leak detectors in storage and operational areas. Tanks should not exceed 85% capacity to allow for gas expansion. Foundation and Anchoring Ensure tanks are placed on a sturdy, level foundation to prevent tilting or movement. Ensure the tanks are anchored securely in areas prone to earthquakes or strong winds. Classified Equipment Use only area classified electric equipment within a safe distance to avoid fire or explosion hazard. Source of
Ignition, Fan Exhaust / non-explosion-proof electrical equipment must not be within 10 ft of the cylinders. Ensure the installation is at least 33 ft away from (the vertical plane beneath) the High-Voltage lines. Signage Display clear warning signs like “No Smoking” and “Flammable Gas” near the tanks.
