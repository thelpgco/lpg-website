Skip to content LPG Insights Home LPG Insights Liquefied Petroleum Gas (LPG) is a portable, clean and efficient energy source which is readily available to consumers around the world. LPG is primarily obtained from natural gas and oil production but is also produced increasingly from renewable sources; its unique properties make it a versatile energy source which can be used in more than 1,000 different applications. The Carbon footprint of LPG is 20% lower than heating oil and
50% lower than coal. LPG is used in combination with an electric motor in hybrids. LPG has lower Co2 emissions and no particulate matter nor soot, as black carbon or soot is the reason for 20% of the global warming. LPG is the ideal partner for localized renewable power generation.
