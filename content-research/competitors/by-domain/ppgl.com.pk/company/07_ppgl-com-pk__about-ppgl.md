Skip to content About PPGL Home About PPGL About PARCO Pearl Gas PARCO Pearl Gas (Private) Limited (PPGL), a wholly-owned subsidiary of PARCO, is a trusted leader in Pakistan’s LPG industry, with decades of experience and proven operational excellence. As the largest LPG marketing company in the country, PPGL distributes over 150,000 metric tons of LPG annually through both cylinder and bulk supply channels. Our extensive nationwide infrastructure includes strategically located
cylinder filling plants, ensuring safe, efficient, and uninterrupted supply to a diverse customer base. We cater to both domestic and commercial sectors, with a strong emphasis on reliability, quality, and service. To meet the growing and specialized needs of our customers, LPG is sourced from multiple local producers and supplemented with imported product as required. This supply strategy ensures consistent availability across Pakistan. Vision To keep the flame of progress and
