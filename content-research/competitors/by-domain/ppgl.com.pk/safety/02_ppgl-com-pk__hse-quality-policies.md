Skip to content HSE & Quality Policies Home HSE & Quality Policies Health, Safety & Environment At PARCO, we believe, Company’s long-term business success depends on our ability to continually improve HSE performance by protecting people, including stakeholders, assets, environment & reputation of the Company. Therefore, towards our Vision of “Zero Harm”, Health, Safety and Environment matters are considered as integral part of our business processes and shall be duly taken into
account for guiding our behaviors, actions and decisions for achieving HSE excellence in all operations covering Hydrocarbons Refining, Transportation, Storage & Marketing. Commitment and Compliance Health, Safety and Environment are considered at par with productivity and profitability, when making business plans and decisions, as we hold ourselves accountable for HSE compliance in our Engineering, Operations, Products & Services. We comply to all applicable national and international
standards, legal (statutory & regulatory) obligations and World’s best practices relevant to Health, Safety and Environment in our areas of operations. The Company shall strive to introduce state of the art technologies, systems & practices to maintain all operational risks in ALARP (As low as Reasonably Practicable) range. We ensure to promote positive & proactive HSE culture, with adherence to this policy, and HSE Management System by employees, contractors, and service providers.
Performance and Risk Management We improve HSE competencies of employees and stakeholders to complement their good performance. Specific HSE goals and objectives are set for all levels of hierarchy to continuously improve the Organization’s HSE performance. We implement Risk Management techniques to identify, assess and mitigate all risks to acceptable levels in our Projects & Operations. We strive to minimize Environmental Impact of our Projects & Operation through Environment
Management System and techniques in compliance to local and International laws. We encourage efficient use of energy and natural resources. Ensure Organizational readiness to manage emergencies / accidents through efficient & effective Emergency & Crisis Management protocols for business continuity and to safe guard Company’s reputation. Focus Areas Ensure & encourage all hazards, near miss & incidents are reported fearlessly in a positive manner, duly investigated & actions generated
are closed meaningfully in set timelines to avoid recurrence. We shall remain focused on health of our employees & contractors through provision of healthy working conditions, work life balance, health promotion initiatives and medical services. We shall strive to further improve our performance of Process Safety & Asset Integrity to control major accident hazards. We actively engage in stakeholder dialogue and manage our social impacts on communities around. Quality Policy PARCO,
towards its Vision of operational excellence, place Quality Assurance & Control as an integral part of Corporate governance through leadership commitment for continual improvement in all its value chains and functions covering Hydrocarbons Refining, Transportation, Storage & Marketing business. Therefore, We commit to: Ensure adherence of this policy and of Quality Management System (QMS) by all stakeholders including employees, contractors, and service providers, Comply with all
applicable statutory and regulatory requirements along with Certification of ISO 9001, Ensure specific key performance indicators and Quality objectives are set for all levels of hierarchy targeting continual process and procedural improvements to evaluate and monitor performance of product and services, Achieve customer satisfaction through stringent quality assurance standards for in-time delivery of products and services, Establish systems to adhere applicable Quality standards
in project life cycle from concept, design, manufacturing, construction to commissioning stages, Follow Risk based approach in decision making & execution process in the Company at each and every level, Develop plausible maintenance management & inspection philosophies and systems to ensure asset integrity in all business. Make certain that the work processes are well defined and documented as well as controlled, protected, accessible and retrievable at all levels Companywide,
Maintain transparent mechanism of both internal and external Quality audits to measure the performance of Company against set targets and objectives, Ensure personal competence of employees is increased by training and awareness programs and performance evaluation against set objectives aligned with Company’s targets, Establish mechanism for reporting of internal & external customers’ feedback on Quality of products and services to the highest level.
