Works 24/7 from your phone. Bank app & internet banking Most bank apps
list SNGPL/SSGC under Utility / Gas bill payments. Add your consumer number once and pay each month in seconds. ATM & over-the-counter At a 1LINK ATM choose Bill Payment → Gas and enter your consumer number, or pay cash at a designated bank branch or franchise. Online portal & 1Bill The official SNGPL/SSGC portal and 1Bill-enabled apps accept card and account payments against your consumer number. Step by step Pay in four steps 1 Open your bill Use the bill check to open your official
SNGPL/SSGC bill and note the amount due, due date, and consumer number. 2 Pick a method JazzCash, EasyPaisa, your bank app, ATM, or in person — all use the same 1Bill / consumer number to find the bill. 3 Enter your consumer number In your chosen app, go to Bill Payments → Gas → SNGPL or SSGC, and enter the consumer number exactly as printed. 4 Confirm and keep the receipt Pay before the due date and save the transaction ID or SMS — it's your proof of payment. FAQ Paying your bill,
answered Can I pay my Sui gas bill with JazzCash or EasyPaisa? Yes. In either app go to Bill Payments → Gas, choose SNGPL or SSGC, enter your 11-digit consumer number, check the amount, and pay. You'll get an instant confirmation SMS as your receipt. How do I find the amount due before I pay? Open your official bill first using the bill check — pick SNGPL or SSGC and enter your consumer number. The portal shows the exact amount due and due date, which every payment method then
matches. Which method confirms the payment fastest? JazzCash, EasyPaisa, and bank-app payments update almost immediately and give you a transaction ID. Always keep that ID or the confirmation SMS until the next bill clears. Does Cylin process the payment? No. Cylin is independent and not affiliated with SNGPL, SSGC, or OGRA.
