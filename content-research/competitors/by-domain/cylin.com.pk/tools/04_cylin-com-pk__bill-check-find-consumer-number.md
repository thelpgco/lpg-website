Sui gas bill — view, download, or pay. FAQ Consumer number, answered Where is the 11-digit consumer number on my SNGPL bill? It is printed at the very top of the bill, labelled Consumer No. / Customer ID, near your name and address. SNGPL numbers are 11 digits long. My SSGC bill number is only 10 digits — is that right? Yes. SSGC (Karachi & south) customer numbers are 10 digits; SNGPL (north Pakistan) consumer numbers are 11. Enter whichever is printed on your bill, digits only.
What if I can't find my paper bill? If you remember the number you can open your bill online directly. Otherwise the number is also on any past bill, SMS, or your gas connection paperwork.
