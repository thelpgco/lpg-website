GALLERY - June 26, 2022 LPG Use for Crop Drying Including Tobacco Did you know that LPG can help dry and cure crops? When it comes to short harvest seasons, efficiency is the key to ensuring a positive impact on the economic bottom line. It is where LPG comes strong! Liquid Petroleum Gas offers versatile heat to dry and cure crops efficiently and accurately. LPG can power the rice, fruit, corn, tobacco, and grain while preserving their monetary value. And LPG can do all of this
regardless of the weather conditions. Liquid Petroleum Gas as Phenomenal Agriculture Crop Drying Solution Whether you are talking about drying grain or fruit, or even tobacco curing, LPG powered drying system is unbeatable. It helps the farmers receive better prices for their hard work while maintaining the moisture content of the crops. Another exciting thing is that 90% of the LPG drawn from the tanks gets converted into energy. It means minimized wastage with cost-efficiency.
Let us know more about LPG application in crop drying. Fruit Drying The fruit drying process revolves around dehydration. Dehydration is the process through which water gets drained out from nuts, vegetables, and fruits at an accelerated speed. It stabilizes the crops with low moisture that refrains them from getting damaged. Here, LPG is the perfect choice to fuel the burners for fruit dehydration. Since LPG is non-toxic, the crops remain safe. Even the emissions from LPG burners
are low compared to oil-fueled burners. The storage capability is also unmatchable, and LPG leakage can never contaminate the surroundings. Grain Drying When it comes to farming operations, grain drying has become an essential part. LPG fuel heat air dry grains eliminating the moisture content. Since LPG is portable, it can be easily stored and readily accessible everywhere; it is becoming the top favorite. Natural gas is falling behind! Another aspect is that, unlike kerosene
and oil, LPG never leaks and contaminates the crops and grains. LPG also limits fungal contact and bacterial growth, making the lives of the farmers better with flexibility. Tobacco Curing Did you know the majority of the bulk-curing barns have been running on LPG burners since the 1970s? Today, the majority of the growers switched from oil to LPG due to the ratio of efficiency and convenience. Liquid Petroleum Gas and the air are best friends and combust perfectly as compared
to oil and the air. As per analysis, a well-operated barn will cure 9 pounds of tobacco leaf per gallon of LPG. Benefits of LPG Usage for Crop Drying and Tobacco Curing LPG is a cost-effective solution for drying and curing crops and tobacco as compared to oil. It uses 50% less thermal energy, making it a highly energy-efficient solution. When crops are dried and cured with LPG, the chances of over-drying decrease. LPG enhances the overall quality of the crops, grains, and tobacco
leaves. The Bottom Line Liquid Petroleum Gas is a genuine choice to dry crops, grains, and tobacco curing. The convenience of usage, benefits and energy efficiency makes LPG a game-changer for farmers.
