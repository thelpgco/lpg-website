GALLERY - December 19, 2022 Preparing LPG users for the Winter Winter has started and so has the need for an affective heating system, which means it’s a good time to think about how you’re going to take the edge off the cold when it really sets in. For most areas in Pakistan warming the inside of the house isn’t the only concern – the gas loadshedding has increased to an extent that it also affects cooking practises in homes. Here’s a few ways you can be prepared this Winter:
Check your gas supply timely You probably filled your tank, but as the temperatures continue to dip, keep a watchful eye on your fuel supply levels. Check them regularly to ensure you’re never below the recommended 25% – so you are never out of fuel!
