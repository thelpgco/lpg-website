GALLERY - February 8, 2023 Autogas- cheaper than Petrol LPG, also known as propane, is a by-product of crude oil extraction and the refining process. Over the past few years, the use of LPG as Autogas; a motor fuel has increased significantly due to its lower cost compared to petrol and its environmentally friendly properties. On average, auto LPG is 20-30% cheaper than petrol, making it an attractive option for those who want to save money on their fuel costs. An overwhelming
majority of Rickshaws and Public Vehicles use Autogas in Pakistan and globally it is the third most widely used fuel after Petrol and Diesel. Private vehicles have begun converting to Autogas as well, as the number of Autogas Stations in Pakistan is increasing. LPG has several other benefits, when compared to petrol. Auto LPG releases fewer emissions and produces lower levels of harmful pollutants such as carbon monoxide and hydrocarbons. This makes it a more sustainable choice
for those who are concerned about the environment and its impact on it. Autogas also has an excellent safety record. LPG has a higher octane rating compared to petrol, which can result in improved acceleration and a smoother driving experience. The octane rating of a fuel refers to its ability to resist knocking or pinging during combustion, which can cause damage to the engine of vehicles. A higher octane rating means that the fuel can withstand higher compression ratios and temperatures,
allowing for a more efficient combustion process. In conclusion, Auto LPG is a cheaper and more environmentally friendly alternative to petrol. The benefits of using auto LPG make it an attractive option for those who want to save money on fuel costs and reduce their impact on the environment!
