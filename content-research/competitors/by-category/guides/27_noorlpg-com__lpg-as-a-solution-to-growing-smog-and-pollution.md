GALLERY - January 6, 2023 LPG as a Solution to Growing Smog and Pollution Air pollution is the greatest environmental threat to public health globally and accounts for an estimated 7 million premature deaths every year. Smog is air pollution that reduces visibility. The term “smog” was first used in the early 1900s to describe a mix of smoke and fog. But what can we do to reduce it? Some would say nothing, but that’s not the case, many factors add up to the smog conditions in the
country, your choice of fuel being one of them. The winter and gas load-shedding calls for other ways of heating and cooking, some go for electric heaters, and some go for coal and wood-based heat. However, all these solutions eventually end up in either a high cost or impure air in the surroundings that eventually affect the overall climate change. Using retrospective, longitudinal data, a study in China concluded that women cooking with cleaner fuels, including LPG, were more
likely to report better health and had a lower probability of chronic and acute diseases when compared to women cooking with biomass fuels.
