About Cylin · Pakistan mein bana, Pakistani gharon ke liye The trust layer for LPG distribution LPG distribution in emerging markets runs on phone calls and WhatsApp — with no verification, no tracking, and a real human cost. Cylin exists to change that. Book a demo Join the team Why we exist We take responsibility for what we facilitate Getting gas to a home should be safe, verifiable, and accountable. Today, in most markets, it isn't. Orders move through group chats, drivers
are untracked, and when something goes wrong there's no record of what happened. We're not “Uber for cylinders.” Cylin is a verification, dispatch, and operations platform that takes responsibility for the deliveries it facilitates — connecting distributors, households, and drivers around one safety-first system. We started in Pakistan, building for one distributor at a time, and we generalize only once we've earned it in the field. What we believe Principles we won't compromise
