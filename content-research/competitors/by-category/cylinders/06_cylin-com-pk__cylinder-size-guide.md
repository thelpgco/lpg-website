FAQ Size ke sawaal Konsa LPG cylinder behtar hai mere ghar ke liye? (Which cylinder size is right?) 5 kg suits a small home, students, or a backup burner; 11.8 kg is the average family domestic size; 45.4 kg is for restaurants,
dhabas, and heavy commercial use. 11.8 kg cylinder kitne din chalega? (How long does an 11.8 kg cylinder last?)
