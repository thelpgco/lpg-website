GALLERY - October 28, 2022 200 KG Tanks- the ideal solution for Industries, Apartment Buildings and Large Scale Commercial Use. People in Pakistan have been suffering every year in the winter due to unavailability of gas – the demand for LPG also increases every winter season, which means one should make preparations well in advance. Noor LPG has recently introduced 200 KG tanks to help and accommodate their customers. These cylinders are now becoming extremely popular specially
for mid-size businesses that have space constraints such as shopping malls as well as apartment buildings. Here’s why you should go for these tanks: Higher Efficiency Buying in bulk makes it easier to access LPG when you need it for your business’s needs. You can easily plan and keep a daily schedule for the usage without getting off track. For example, if your business is operating a food court, an on-site storage tank ensures a quick refueling process, so your facility runs according
to schedule whether you operate daily, nightly or 24/7. Reliability and Accessibility Having a tank of 200 kg makes it easier for you to access LPG, you don’t have to worry about the daily fluctuations in costs or even unavailability of smaller cylinders of LPG. Moreover, it is easier to refill these cylinders at your convenience and at your location instead of having to constantly remove empty cylinders and replace them with filled ones. Decreased Downtime For businesses – buying
LPG in bulk can decrease the company’s downtime and improve productivity by saving time, money and effort of frequent refills. This way you are skipping the part where you are running out of LPG and must wait for your next delivery every few days.
